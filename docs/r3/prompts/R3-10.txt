Task:
Repo sitio (Next.js 15, TypeScript, Drizzle, MySQL/MariaDB). Ticket R3-10, from docs/r3/README.md Batch 2. Add a free-trial promotion that the superadmin switches on and off. When it is on, a business that signs up through /registro (ticket R3-9, merged) gets a trial subscription automatically, the landing page shows a promotion banner, and the existing subscription lifecycle (grace, then paused) takes over when the trial ends.
